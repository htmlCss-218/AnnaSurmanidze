What is a Galaxy? A galaxy is a massive collection of stars, gas, dust, and dark matter, held together by gravity. Our galaxy, the Milky Way, is just one of billions in the universe. Galaxies come in various shapes, such as spiral, elliptical, and irregular. They are constantly moving, with some even colliding over time. Studying galaxies helps us understand the universe’s formation and the mysteries of dark matter.
